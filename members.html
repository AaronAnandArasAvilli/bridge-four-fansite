Important Members

Learn about some of the important members of Bridge Four.

Kaladin

Kaladin becomes one of the most important leaders connected to Bridge Four. Before becoming a bridge leader, he experienced life as a soldier and later became a bridge runner.

Kaladin helps the members of Bridge Four work together and encourages them to protect one another.

Role in Bridge Four

  • Leader and protector
  • Helps organize Bridge Four
  • Encourages teamwork and loyalty

Teft

Teft is an experienced member of Bridge Four who becomes an important mentor and friend to the group.

His knowledge and experience help the younger members of Bridge Four understand their responsibilities.

Role in Bridge Four

  • Experienced member
  • Mentor to other members
  • Supports Bridge Four

Lopen

Lopen is one of the memorable members of Bridge Four. He is known for his humor and unique personality.

Lopen becomes an important part of the group and develops strong relationships with the other members.

Role in Bridge Four

  • Bridge Four member
  • Provides humor and personality
  • Supports his fellow members
