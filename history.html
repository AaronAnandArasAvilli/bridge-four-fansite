History of Bridge Four

From a bridge crew to an important group.

Life as a Bridge Crew

Bridge Four originally served as one of the bridge crews of the armies of the Alethi. Bridge crews were assigned dangerous work during battles.

The members of Bridge Four faced difficult conditions and had to work together to survive.

Changes Under Kaladin

Under Kaladin's leadership, Bridge Four began to change. The members developed greater trust and started working together as a team.

Their relationships and teamwork became an important part of the group's identity.

Becoming More Than a Bridge Crew

Bridge Four eventually became much more than a group of bridge runners. Its members took on new roles and responsibilities.

The group became known for loyalty, courage, and supporting one another.

Legacy

Bridge Four becomes an important symbol of teamwork and unity throughout the story.

Its members continue to develop while maintaining their connection to the group.
