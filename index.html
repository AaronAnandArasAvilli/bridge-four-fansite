What is Bridge Four?

Bridge Four is a bridge crew from Brandon Sanderson's The Stormlight Archive. The group begins as a bridge crew and develops into an important group of characters throughout the story.

Bridge Four becomes known for its loyalty, teamwork, courage, and strong relationships between its members.
